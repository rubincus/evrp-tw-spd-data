# Runs and best solutions for the electric vehicle routing problem with time windows, simultaneous pickup and delivery, and partial recharges

Data generated by a cooperative tabu search that runs on a graphics processing unit (GPU) for the **electric vehicle
routing problem with time windows, simultaneous pickup and delivery, and partial recharges (EVRP-TW-SPD)**: the runs
of its computational study and the best solutions found. The best solutions improve the best published value of
20 of the 20 jd instances (20 of them with fewer vehicles) and of 40 of the 56 akb
instances with 100 customers (11 with fewer vehicles). The code is kept in a separate repository.

**Snapshot of 2026-10-31.** Final campaign complete.

> **Resumen en español.** Este repositorio contiene los datos del estudio computacional de una búsqueda tabú
> cooperativa en GPU para este problema: una fila por ejecución en `runs/<experimento>/runs.csv` con la salida de cada
> ejecución (parámetros, traza y rutas), las mejores soluciones encontradas en las dos convenciones (`bks/` y
> `bks_hma_graph/`), su
> verificación con la evaluación de rutas del código de HMA, los costos de las ejecuciones del código público de HMA en
> la misma máquina y con el mismo tiempo, y la tabla `best_values.csv` con el mejor valor por instancia. Las tablas de
> abajo resumen los valores encontrados.

## Contents

| Path | Content |
|---|---|
| `best_values.csv` | One row per instance: HMA's best published value, the best published value, the best value found with its gap, status and file, and the best value found over HMA's graph. |
| `bks/` | Best solution found in any run for every instance whose best published value was improved during the study, `<instance>.txt` in HMA's solution format with the stations traversed by an arc written as visits; `history.csv` lists every improvement of this archive. |
| `bks_hma_graph/` | Best solution found over HMA's own graph for every instance where it improves HMA's best value, with its `history.csv`. |
| `runs/<experiment>/` | `runs.csv`, one row per run (columns below), and the output of every run: `<base>.json` with the parameters, the best solution and the trace per epoch, and `<base>.txt` with the routes of the best solution. |
| `hma_equal_time/` | Runs of HMA's public code on the same machine with the same time budgets (costs only). |
| `verification/hma_check.txt` | Route evaluation of HMA's own code on every file of `bks/` and `bks_hma_graph/`. |
| `analysis/arc_characterization.csv` | Per jd instance: share of the arcs evaluated by the search that HMA's graph makes longer than the direct arc, their mean increase, the share of arcs of the dominant graph shorter than the direct arc and their mean decrease, and the best solutions evaluated over HMA's arcs. |
| `analysis/jd_structure.csv` | Per jd instance: violations of the triangle inequality in time and in distance (%), and statistics of HMA's station shortcuts. |

The repository contains only results produced by our runs. Instances, solutions of other methods and code are not
included. The instances are available in the public repository of HMA,
[0SliverBullet/EVRP-TW-SPD-HMA](https://github.com/0SliverBullet/EVRP-TW-SPD-HMA).

## Benchmarks, objective and charging policy

* **akb** (Akbay et al., 2023): 36 instances with 5, 10 or 15 customers and 56 with 100 customers and 21 stations,
  derived from the instances of Schneider et al. (2014). Euclidean distances, travel times equal to distances.
  Cost = 1000 K + distance.
* **jd** (Zheng et al., 2025): 20 instances with 200, 400, 600, 800 and 1000 customers and 100 stations, with road
  distances and travel times. Cost = 300 K + 0.014 distance.

K is the number of vehicles. At a station the vehicle charges the minimum energy needed to reach the next station or
the depot, and charges longer when the following customers impose a waiting time (the policy of HMA, Zheng et al.,
2025).

## Conventions

* **Main (dominant graph G').** Each arc is the shortest path through stations, traversed without recharging, that is
  not slower than the direct arc. In the files of `bks/` the stations traversed by an arc are written as explicit
  visits, so the routes can be evaluated over the direct arcs of the instance.
* **HMA's graph (G_H).** Each arc is the fastest path through stations, the graph searched by HMA. Solutions in
  `bks_hma_graph/` and in `runs/final_strict/`.

On the akb instances both graphs coincide with the direct arcs.

## File formats

* `bks/*.txt`, `bks_hma_graph/*.txt`: the solution format of HMA's code, one line per route with its cost and its
  nodes (0 is the depot; depot and stations carry the battery levels written by that format), followed by the number
  of vehicles and the total cost.
* `runs/*/<base>.txt`: a header with instance, seed, cost, vehicles, distance and the time at which the best solution
  was found, then one route per line as node identifiers of the instance over the search graph of the run (explicit
  station visits are listed; stations traversed by an arc are not).
* `runs/*/<base>.json`: the same run with its parameters and the trace per epoch.

## Verification

Every solution was evaluated with an independent implementation of HMA's evaluation rules, which reproduces the cost
and feasibility of the best solutions published by HMA. The files of `bks/` and `bks_hma_graph/` were also evaluated
with the route evaluation of HMA's own code (`verification/hma_check.txt`): 81 of the 81 files were checked; all their routes are feasible and their costs match.

## Experiments

| Experiment | Content | Runs | Instances | Seeds | Configurations | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `final` | Main convention (dominant graph G'): every instance, final configuration | 1120 | 112 | 1-10 | 1 | Comparison with the best published values and with HMA at equal time, convergence figure, supplementary tables |
| `final_strict` | HMA's graph G_H: the jd instances, same configuration and budgets | 60 | 20 | 1-3 | 1 | Comparison of the search graphs (table and figure), supplementary jd table |
| `design` | Design study of the whole-instance search: one parameter or component changed at a time | 345 | 10 | 1-3 | 27 | Ablation and sensitivity tables |
| `design_decomp` | Decomposition settings and ablations on two jd instances with 400 customers | 32 | 2 | 1-3 | 8 | Ablation and sensitivity tables |
| `cpu_pilot` | CPU version: number of trajectories B on two jd instances with 200 customers | 10 | 2 | 1 | 5 | Choice of B for the CPU version |
| `cpu_final` | CPU version on four performance cores: cpu_tuned (B = 32) and cpu_same (B = 512) | 112 | 14 | 1-5 | 2 | Comparison of the algorithm on the CPU and on the GPU |

Budgets are one sixth of HMA's long time limit: 600, 1800, 3000, 4200 and 5400 s for the jd instances with 200 to 1000
customers, 105 s for the akb instances with 100 customers and 20 s for the small ones (design study: 600 s on jd200
and 105 s on akb). All runs used an Intel Core i9-14900K and an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 under Windows 11.

**Columns of `runs.csv`.** `tag` experiment; `config` configuration; `graph` search graph (`shortest`: dominant graph
G', `shortcuts`: HMA's graph, `original`: direct arcs); `instance`, `seed`; `tmax` and `budget` time budget (s);
`n_ext` budget extensions (0); `cost`, `K`, `distance` of the best solution; `feasible`; `t_best` time at which it was
found (s); `best_at_90` best cost at 90% of the budget; `improve_last10_pct` improvement during the last 10% of the
budget (%); `iterations`; `cost_600` to `cost_3000` best cost after that many seconds; `hma_best` HMA's best published
value and `gap_hma_best` (%); `hma_mean_T1`, `hma_mean_T2` HMA's published mean costs with its short and long time
limits and their gaps; `hma_direct` HMA's best solution with its station detours replaced by direct arcs and
`gap_direct`; `new_best` the run improved the archive; `overrides` configuration changes; `date` end of the run;
`file` base name of the output files.

**Columns of `hma_equal_time/*.csv`.** `mode`: `default` (HMA's stopping rule, 20 generations without improvement) or
`full` (whole budget); `seed`, `budget` (s); `cost` and `K` reported by HMA's code; `time` (s); `feasible`;
`verified_cost` recomputed with our evaluator; `date`. HMA was compiled with the optimization level of its build
command and run with the settings of its documentation, four runs at a time, each pinned to a performance core. The
default-mode run of jd600_4 with seed 8 crashes reproducibly and was replaced by seed 11.
